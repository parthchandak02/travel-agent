### Reservation Policy
- **Reservation required** — NOT walk-in friendly
- Book online at booking.refuge.com (create account first)
- Same-day reservations accepted by availability (real-time online system)
- 24-hr cancellation policy (full charge within 24h)
- 30-min grace period for late arrival
- Credit card required to hold reservation; paid at check-in
- Cashless facility (cards, Apple/Google Pay, gift cards only)

### What's Included
- Himalayan salt wall sauna
- Eucalyptus steam rooms
- Hot & warm pools (cascading thermal waterfalls)
- Nordic cool & cold pools
- Outdoor fire pits with Adirondack chairs
- Day-use robe rental
- Bottled water
- Secure locker + towels

### Booking Urgency
Summer weekends are the most popular time. At ~2 weeks out for Aug 1 (Sat) or Aug 2 (Sun), book ASAP. Check booking.refuge.com for green (available) vs grey (sold out) dates.

### Note on FAQ vs Booking Hours

But the **live booking system sidebar** says:
- Mon–Thu 9AM–10PM, Fri–Sun 8AM–10PM
The booking system is the operational source of truth — summer hours are likely extended.

## Monterey Bay Aquarium

**📍** 886 Cannery Row, Monterey, CA | **📞** (831) 648-4800
**🔗** tickets.montereybayaquarium.org (actual ticketing subdomain)

### Pricing (confirmed from official site, Summer 2026)

| Type | Price |
|------|-------|
| **Adult** (18–69) | **$65** |
| Youth (5–17) | $50 |
| Senior (70+) | $50 |
| Children 4 & under | Free |

### Hours
- Currently **10AM–5PM** (as of Jul 2026; some sources say 10AM–6PM in peak summer)
- Hours vary by date — check the schedule page for specific date
- **Last entry:** 1 hour before closing
- No time limit once inside
- Re-entry allowed with handstamp at exit

### Ticket Policies
- **Advance purchase strongly recommended** for smooth entry
- **Tickets on sale through September 7, 2026** — Jul 31 and Aug 1 are in the sales window
- Must buy through official site only (tickets.montereybayaquarium.org) — no third-party sales
- **Non-refundable** but exchangeable until 4PM PT on ticket date (subject to availability)
- Exchange online via account or call (831) 648-4800 option 3
- Free for SNAP EBT/CalFresh cardholders (up to 4 people) — Museums for All program

### Booking Process
1. Go to tickets.montereybayaquarium.org
2. Create account / log in (redirects to login immediately)
3. Select date and ticket quantity
4. Optional: add tours (Aquarium Past & Present $29/person, Family Adventures $35/person)
5. Checkout
