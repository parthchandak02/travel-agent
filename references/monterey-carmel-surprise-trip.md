## Peak Season Pricing Reality

Peak summer pricing (July-August in coastal CA) can be **2x** the off-season rates found in research. Always run hotel-goat live for specific dates rather than relying on aggregate site estimates.

## Activities & Field Notes

### Point Lobos State Natural Reserve
- Day use does NOT require advance reservation
- Parking: first-come, max 150 vehicles
- Arrive before 9:30AM or after 3PM to avoid "one-in, one-out" policy on summer weekends
- $10 entry fee per vehicle
- Closes at 7PM, last entry 6:30PM
- Pilot reservation system being tested — check parks.ca.gov/pointlobos before trip
- No dogs allowed
- Best trails: Bird Island Trail + China Cove loop (~2 miles)

### Activities — Verified Pricing
- Tandem kayak Lovers Point: $64.20 (2hr) — adventuresbythesea.com
- Guided coastal bike tour from Lovers Point: $48.15/person ($96.30 for two) — 2hr, 10AM or 2PM
- Self-guided bike rental: $32.10 half-day, $42.80 full-day
- Surrey bike rental: $74.20 (2hr) — "romantic outing for two"
- Monterey Bay Aquarium: $65/adult — buy online in advance
- Escape Room 831: $60-80
- Point Lobos: $10/vehicle — first-come, no reservation needed
- Carmel Beach + village: free

## Booking System Quirks
- **Four Sisters Collection hotels** (Green Gables, Coachman's Inn): booking widget requires full form submission + JS. Cannot scrape programmatically.

- **Horizon Inn**: rezStream engine. Direct availability works via browser.
- **Spindrift Inn**: Book direct at spindriftinn.com or via OTAs. Summer avg $536/night weekend.
